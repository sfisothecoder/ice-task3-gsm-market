WEDE5020 POE - Part 1
Proposal 1: GSM Market Website Project Proposal
1. Organisation Overview
I have chosen GSM Market because it is a small retail business that sells different products such as
mobile phones, phone accessories, electronics, clothing, and other everyday items. Many small
businesses today use social media to advertise their products, but not every business has its own
website. I think having a website would make GSM Market look more professional and help
customers find information more easily. The website will give customers one place where they can
learn about the business, see the products that are available, and find contact details. Instead of
searching through many social media posts, customers will be able to visit the website to find the
information they need. I believe this will improve communication between the business and its
customers.
2. Website Goals and Objectives
The main goal of this project is to create a simple website using HTML. The website will introduce
GSM Market and provide useful information for customers. The objectives are to introduce the
business to new customers, display the products that GSM Market sells, provide contact details,
help customers learn more about the business, make the business easier to find online, and create
a website that is simple, organised, and easy to use.
3. Current Website Analysis
At the moment, GSM Market does not have an official website. Customers mainly use social media
to see products and contact the business. This can sometimes make it difficult to find information
because older posts are not always easy to find. Having a website will solve this problem because
customers will have one place where they can always find the latest business information.
4. Proposed Website Features
The website will include a Home page, About Us page, Products page, and Contact Us page. The
Home page will welcome visitors and explain what the business sells. The About Us page will tell
customers about the history of GSM Market. The Products page will include pictures and short
descriptions of different products. The Contact Us page will include the business phone number,
email address, physical address, and business hours.
5. Design and User Experience
Since I have only learned HTML, I want to keep the website simple and easy to use. Every page will
have clear headings, paragraphs, pictures, lists, and navigation links at the top so users can move
between pages easily. The information will be organised so customers can quickly find what they
are looking for.
6. Technical Requirements
The website will be created using HTML5 in Visual Studio Code. Images will be added to make the
website more attractive. GitHub will be used to save my project files and keep track of my work.
7. Timeline
Week 1: Research the business and collect information.
Week 2: Plan the website and decide on the pages.
Week 3: Create the HTML pages.
Week 4: Add text, images, and navigation links.
Week 5: Test all the pages and correct any mistakes.
